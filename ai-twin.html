AI twin questions

What is an AI twin?

An AI twin is an AI version of a real person: it looks and sounds like them and answers questions the way they would, from what they've shared. This one lives on your website as a video call that visitors can join.

How is an AI twin different from a chatbot?

Visitors see your face and hear your voice, they can talk out loud and interrupt it like a person, and it only answers from your own material instead of the whole internet.

Will my AI twin make things up?

It's instructed to answer only from what you've written and to say when it doesn't know, pointing visitors to your email. Like any AI it can still get things wrong, and it says it's an AI when asked.

Is an AI twin a deepfake?

It's only ever made of you, from photos and a voice sample you provide, and it tells visitors it's an AI version of you whenever they ask.
