It's built on the $P point-cloud recognizer, modified to work in 3D. In VR you hold the index trigger, draw a gesture in the air, and on release it tries to recognize it. There are no built-in gestures: you record your own templates in an add mode. A dev version lets you author and save templates to a file, and a game version just loads them. It's on GitHub as VR-gesture-recognition-godot, with a demo on YouTube.
