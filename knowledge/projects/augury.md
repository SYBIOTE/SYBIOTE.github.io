# AUGURY: Duel of the Gods

AUGURY is a turn-based mind-game fighter I made for the DreamLayer Jam in October 2026, in the spirit of Your Only Move Is HUSTLE. Gods from different pantheons, like Anubis and Raijin, duel in a 2D arena. Both players pick their moves at the same time, and each exchange plays out as freeze-frame poses, so winning is about reading your opponent, not reaction speed.

A few systems make the reads interesting: arenas announce their hazards ahead of time, so you can plan around them; each god has a Domain that rewrites the arena; and correctly calling your opponent's move earns Divinity and a counter-hit.

The character art was generated and then iteratively edited with DreamLayer, which is what kept each god consistent across all their poses. It's playable on my itch.io, sybiote dot itch dot io, and there's a gameplay video on YouTube.
