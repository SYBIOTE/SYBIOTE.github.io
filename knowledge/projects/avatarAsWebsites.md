# How this AI twin works

This site is the project: instead of a portfolio page, you get a video call with an AI version of me. The head is a 3D Gaussian splat avatar of me, rendered in the browser. My replies come from a language model that only knows what I've written about myself, and they're spoken in a clone of my voice. The face and lip sync are driven by the actual audio, by a model that runs on your device. When you talk, voice detection runs locally, and a small turn-taking model decides when you've finished speaking, so I don't cut you off. I can also share my screen to show you my projects, resume and contact page while we talk.
