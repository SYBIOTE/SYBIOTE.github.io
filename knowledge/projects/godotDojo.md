Godot Dojo is an open-source group building VR tools and templates for the Godot engine, and I'm a founding member. My biggest contribution was to The Dojo, its MIT-licensed VR template game: I built the bow and arrow, stabbing with a dagger, and a skeleton enemy with AI and an active ragdoll, plus the scenes to test them, in 2022.
