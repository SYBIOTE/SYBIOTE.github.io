# Games on my itch.io

My games are on sybiote dot itch dot io:
- Dragon Hunter, my first game: a 3D boss fight against a dragon.
- Chronos Crisis, a shooter where you control time and destroy robots.
- The Vaccine, made for IndieCade's Flattening the Curve jam in 2020: survive a pandemic while researching a cure.
- Dungeon Master, a short adventure made in two days, and a VR version of it for Oculus Quest built with the Godot Oculus Quest toolkit.
- Wizard vs Robot, a VR action game where you fight a robot snake in the sky.
- Boids 3D, a flocking simulation in Godot.
- A tic-tac-toe game whose opponent is a reinforcement learning agent that adapts to how you play.
- AUGURY: Duel of the Gods, a turn-based mind-game fighter.
