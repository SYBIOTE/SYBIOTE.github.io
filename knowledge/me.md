# About me

I work on AI and XR: I like bending reality with code and coffee, and lately I build AI avatars in XR, like the one you're talking to. I started out making VR games in Godot in college, where I founded the game development and XR club and was a founding member of Godot Dojo. From 2023 I moved to the 3D web, building the editor and visual scripting of an open-source WebXR engine, Ethereal Engine, which became IR Engine. These days I'm deep in AI for 3D characters: auto-rigging, motion generation and animation tools in the browser.
