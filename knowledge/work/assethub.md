# The company and my role

AssetHub is an AI-powered 3D platform, a Techstars company: an AI-native 3D editor that takes you from concept art to production-ready 3D models, with AI mesh generation and texture refinement. Around when I joined, it had raised a seed round of about two million dollars. I was a full-stack engineer there from September 2025 to September 2026.

# What I built

I built customer-facing workflows and internal tooling for generating and processing 3D assets with AI, and worked across the React and TypeScript interfaces and the backend services behind the core 3D editor. The editor takes a team from concept art to a production-ready model in one place: generate a mesh with AI, refine its textures and keep iterating without leaving the browser. We shipped fast and iterated with customers, and in 8 months the platform grew to more than 11,000 users and $5K in monthly recurring revenue.
